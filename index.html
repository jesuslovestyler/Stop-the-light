How it scores

One light races around the ring of 48 bulbs. Freeze it on a gold bulb at the top to clear the round.

Clearing round N pays N × 100 points. You have 3 tries per game — a miss costs one, and the game tells you exactly how many bulbs away you landed.

Rounds 1–4: gold zone is 3 bulbs · Rounds 5–7: 2 bulbs · Round 8 and up: a single bulb. The light starts at 1.2 laps a second and speeds up every round.

Play money: none. Just you and the racing light.
